## Privacy, safety, and legal-content caveats

- The instructions ask the user to provide full legal name, consumer email(s), and state/country, stored in `~/.privacy-rights/profile.yaml`; they say not to commit it. Request metadata is stored in `~/.privacy-rights/requests.json`. The source is text-only and does not establish encryption, restrictive file permissions, backup exclusion, or retention enforcement.
- Positive guardrails include requiring explicit approval of the rendered message before sending, pausing for CAPTCHA/identity verification, not acting as an authorized-agent service, and warning about irreversible deletion/export sensitivity. These are prompt instructions, not enforced product controls.
- The package includes statute citations, deadline claims and privacy-request advice. Those legal assertions were not independently verified against current primary legal sources in this software review; do not treat this candidate as a legal authority.
- Discover is described as scanning email metadata, but it is not included in source/dist. No email account, Gmail MCP, browser session, external privacy-registry, or recipient was accessed.
